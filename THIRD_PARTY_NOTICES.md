# Third-party notices

## Open-source foundation

Truss-Vision is being developed as a fork of [VictorKaraboychev/statics-simulator](https://github.com/VictorKaraboychev/statics-simulator), an MIT-licensed truss simulator and optimizer.

The upstream copyright and license notices must remain with the fork. Truss-Vision adds image-based scene extraction, Gemma review, uncertainty warnings, and the Halloween-themed review experience.

## Teammate reference implementation

The Mark V implementation supplied by the team is retained under `frontend/legacy-mark-v/` as an internal migration reference. Its upload flow, scene ideas, fixtures, and visual concepts are being adapted into the fork; it is not represented as the upstream open-source foundation.
